Getting started

Pick an app from the tabs at the top. On a phone the tabs scroll sideways. Every page reads the same data, collected from the WarEra API every hour.

  • Filters sit at the top of each page: date range (with quick buttons such as 7 days or 30 days), alliance, country, military unit and player. Typing part of a name offers matches.
  • Cards or Table (top right) switches how tables look on a phone. Cards show one row per card; Table keeps the columns (scroll sideways). Your choice is remembered on that device.
  • Sorting: on a computer, tap a column heading (tap again to reverse). On a phone, use the Sort by menu above the table where there is one.
  • Download CSV above a table saves exactly what the table shows, for a spreadsheet.
  • Tap a row where a hint says so, to open details (a player's battles, a citizen's daily donations, a player's skills).
  • An amber banner means the data is more than 2 hours old: the hourly update was late or failed. The Status page says which.

WarEra Tracker

Battles involving The Emerald Accord since 10 November 2026: who dealt damage and who earned battle money (bounty).

Damage tab

  • Choose a period and, optionally, a country, military unit or player. The totals and charts follow the selection.
  • Damage per country (bar chart): damage, coin, or coin per 1M damage for each alliance nation, over the battles you pick, filterable by where the battles were fought.
  • Battles table: each battle with its sides and totals. Copy copies the battle id; Open opens the battle in WarEra.
  • Player card (when a player is selected): their bounty per 1M damage for the period. Show battles lists every battle they fought with damage, money and bounty per 1M, sortable.

Bounty per 1M damage tab

  • One row per alliance country (or military unit): damage, money earned, and money per 1M damage, split by attacker and defender side.
  • With a player, unit or country selected, a by-day chart shows how their bounty per 1M damage changed.
  • Compare players: type up to three names to compare them over the same period.

Company tax tracker

For a host country (any Emerald Accord country where companies stand) and a source country (where their owners are citizens, from any country, or All owners): the income tax the host collects on those companies' wages.

  • Per hour, right now: wages paid, employee and manager tax, from the game's own live wage figures.
  • With All owners chosen, the figures should match the game's own totals for the host closely: it is the quickest way to check the data.
  • Cross-check against the game's region totals, an hour-by-hour list, a day-by-day table and this month's bill (tax owed so far, and a full-month estimate).
  • By region and the list of active companies.
  • What if the tax rates changed? Type a different employee or manager income tax rate to see how the host's daily and monthly income would change, for the companies shown and for the whole country. It assumes companies stay put, so treat big changes as a rough guide.
  • Country income per day (at the end): the host country's whole income right now by stream (employee and manager income tax, development, strategic resources bonus, government type bonus, and a market tax estimate), then day by day for the last 30 days, and the same for every alliance nation.

The game gives income as an hourly rate: per day is that rate × 24, and a past day is the average of that day's checks × 24. Market tax isn't reported by the game: the estimate is the country's market tax rate × the average daily value of market trades its citizens took part in over the last 7 days. It's a rough guide, because the game doesn't say which side's country collects it.

EA Money Transfers

Transfers between nations

  • Country treasury transfers between alliance nations in the period: total received per nation, number of transfers, and battles the nation fought.
  • Tap a nation to list only its transfers. The individual list sorts by newest, oldest, largest, smallest, sender or receiver.

Citizen donations

  • Donations citizens made to their own nation's treasury: total, number of donations, citizens, average per citizen.
  • By nation with each nation's share of everything donated in the table (shares add up to 100% and follow your filters). Tap a nation to filter to it.
  • Citizens: donated, number of donations, average donation, days donated, last donation. Filter by name or by a minimum amount. Tap a citizen for their donations day by day.

Transfers and donations go back 90 days. A single donation is capped at 200 by the game.

Player efficiency

For each alliance nation, its top 20 players with at least 1M damage in the last 7 days, ranked by how much wealth they have, or gained, for the damage they deal.

  • Wealth gained per 1M damage: wealth now minus wealth about a week ago, divided by damage in millions over the same days. Wealth per 1M damage: current wealth divided by damage in millions.
  • Build comes from how the player spent skill points. A skill at level L costs 1 + 2 + … + L points. The share in the four economy skills (companies, energy, management, production) decides it: War 20% or less, Hybrid in between, Eco more than 50%.
  • ↻ reset or ↻ was War next to a build: the player reset their skills in the last 7 days, and what their build was before.
  • Tap a player for their skill levels and, once a few days exist, daily charts of wealth and damage. Filter by nation, military unit, build or name.

Wealth history started on 26 November 2026: until a week exists, changes cover fewer days and damage uses the game's own weekly figure. Wealth is the game's player wealth ranking value; the game doesn't say exactly what it counts. Only each nation's top 100 players by this week's damage are followed.

Status

Whether everything is running, and whether the Company tax tracker matches the game: per alliance country, the tracker's companies, workers and wage tax against the game's own figures, and how that coverage moved over the last week. Also: the age of the data, the last hourly refresh and where it spent its time, the 15-minute battle checks, the last backup and weekly digest, and how much history is stored. A green tick is fine, an amber ! needs a look soon, a red ✕ means something has stopped.

Where the data comes from, and its limits

  • All figures come from the official WarEra API. A full update runs every hour; new and ended battles are checked every 15 minutes.
  • Battle rankings list about the top 100 per side, so a small contributor in a big battle may be missing.
  • Only battles involving an Emerald Accord country are tracked, from 10 November 2026.
  • The company tax tracker follows every company standing in an Emerald Accord country, whoever owns it. Where each company stands is checked daily, so a new, moved or closed company can take up to a day (a move up to a week) to show.
  • Data is kept for 90 days.

Discord alerts

  • New battle involving the alliance, and battle won / lost when it ends, each with a link.
  • Money: a transfer between alliance nations of 5,000 or more, a citizen giving their nation 5,000 or more in one day, a treasury falling by 5,000 or more and by half within 24 hours.
  • Weekly digest on Mondays: this week against last week, top countries and players, bounty per 1M damage, player efficiency, transfers and donations.

Glossary

Bounty, battle money
Money earned in battles, from the game's battle money rankings.
Bounty per 1M damage
Money earned ÷ damage in millions. Higher means more money for the same fighting.
Attacker / defender damage
Damage dealt while fighting on that side of a battle. A country's citizens can fight on both sides.
Host / source country
Host: where a company stands and pays wage tax. Source: the owner's citizenship.
Treasury
A country's money, as the game reports it.
Share (donations)
A nation's part of everything donated in the table, in %.
Build: War / Hybrid / Eco
How much of a player's skill points are in the four economy skills: 20% or less, in between, or more than 50%.
